# Gradlae Founder Video and Demo Script

## One-Line Pitch

Gradlae is building an AI academic ecosystem for university students that turns transcripts, degree requirements, course data, advising knowledge, and campus opportunities into one personalized action plan.

## Founder Video Script: 90 Seconds

Hi, I am the founder of Gradlae.

I am building Gradlae because I lived this problem as a student, and then heard it again from other students while working as a tutor. Students are not struggling only because classes are hard. They are struggling because the university experience is fragmented. Degree requirements are in one place, course catalogs are somewhere else, advising appointments can take weeks, and opportunities like tutoring, clubs, research, and events are scattered across campus websites.

The result is that students lose time. They do not always know what course to take next, whether they are on track to graduate, which credits count, or which resources could actually help them.

Gradlae turns that confusion into an action plan.

Students upload their transcript, and Gradlae maps completed courses, credits, prerequisites, and graduation requirements using real university data. From there, it helps students plan courses, understand what is missing, ask academic questions, and discover relevant opportunities across campus.

For universities, Gradlae is not just another student portal. It is a student-success layer. It reduces repetitive advising questions, gives advisors better context, and helps institutions see where students are getting stuck.

We are starting with a focused academic-planning and advising pilot, because that is painful, measurable, and tied directly to retention and graduation. The long-term vision is bigger: Gradlae becomes the AI academic ecosystem every university student opens to know what to do next.

## Demo Video Script: 2 Minutes

Start on the landing page.

This is Gradlae, an AI academic ecosystem for university students. The core idea is simple: students should not have to search five portals and wait weeks for guidance just to understand what to do next.

Move to the student demo.

Here is the student dashboard. The student starts by uploading a transcript or connecting verified academic data. Gradlae reads completed courses, grades, credits, and transfer history.

Point to credit and requirement tracking.

Gradlae then maps that record against real university requirements. The student can see what is complete, what is missing, and what could block graduation.

Open the course-planning area.

Instead of giving a generic course list, Gradlae recommends a practical semester plan based on prerequisites, requirement categories, course availability, and the student's goals. If a decision is high-risk or uncertain, it is marked for advisor review.

Open the AI advisor.

Now the student can ask academic questions in plain English: "Can I graduate by Spring 2027?" or "What courses should I take if I want to add a minor?" Gradlae answers using university-specific data and shows the reasoning behind the recommendation.

Move to opportunities.

Gradlae also connects students to the rest of campus life. Based on the student's major, interests, and academic path, it recommends tutoring, clubs, events, research opportunities, and other resources.

Show advisor or staff angle.

For advisors and student-success teams, Gradlae creates a clearer view of student needs: common questions, requirement gaps, overloaded plans, resource demand, and cases that need human attention.

Close.

The first pilot is intentionally focused: one department, one set of requirements, real students, and measurable outcomes. We want to prove that Gradlae can improve planning clarity, reduce repetitive advising work, and help students discover the resources they need at the right time.

## YC-Style Closing Line

Universities already have the data and resources students need. Gradlae is the layer that turns all of it into action.

## Demo Talking Points for Funders

- Start narrow: transcript-aware planning and AI academic guidance.
- Prove value with one department before pitching the whole campus.
- Measure advisor load reduction, student planning clarity, and resource discovery.
- Keep advisors in control with review and escalation.
- Expand from planning into tutoring, research, clubs, events, and student-success operations.
